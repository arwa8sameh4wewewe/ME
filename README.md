# ME
About me 
Welcome to Arwa's Space This is a cozy, personal digital corner created by Arwa Sameh Mahmoud, a 16-year-old student and creator living in Cairo, Egypt. Her webpage acts as a creative portfolio that mirrors her real personality, blending her strong academic dedication at OLS Al-hourya school with a lifelong love for drawing that started when she was just six and has grown into crafting awesome 3D sketches.

Aside from her artistic talent, the space takes you deep into Arwa's vibrant world of K-pop. As a passionate fan, she shares her heartfelt love for BTS—celebrating their warm friendships, inspiring messages, and special shoutouts to every single member—alongside her newer excitement for the group NOWz and their hit track "HomeRun," capturing her genuine joy for their music and performances.

The project also marks an exciting turning point in Arwa's life: her first steps into coding. Though she initially didn't care much for it through school, everything changed when she joined the Egyptian government's DECI course and iSchool. With some friendly encouragement and hands-on learning, she picked up HTML and CSS, discovering a real talent and interest in building websites.

Ultimately, Arwa's Space brings all these unique pieces of her life together through a clean design, aesthetic cards, and custom tags ranging from Python and flowcharts to her favorite fandoms. It stands as a wonderful digital identity that celebrates her growth, creativity, and the start of an inspiring new journey.
